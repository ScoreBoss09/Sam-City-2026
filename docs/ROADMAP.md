# Sam City roadmap (the owner's wish list, kept in the repo so work can carry on across sessions)

Targets: Steam, PS5, Xbox. Keep it controller-first, fast, and data-driven so it is easy to mod.
Status: [ ] to do, [~] partly done, [x] done (see notes).

## Transport
1. [x] Stations where sims wait, board a carriage, ride and get off at their stop (rail, monorail, cable car).
2. [x] See into carriages (sims visible inside); Sam rides inside and can walk about the carriage.
3. [x] Track builder like RCT3: lay the line piece by piece (straights, curves, slopes) instead of a straight line station to station.
4. [x] Pillars only on empty ground (never through roads, scenery or buildings).
5. [x] Horse carts and cars stopping dead/glitching when they meet: fix the traffic (queueing, overtaking, passing).
6. [x] Real cars: sims and Sam get in and drive; parking outside homes, garages and car parks; nothing vanishes.
7. [x] Dangerous and drunk driving, police involvement.

## Town life
8. [x] Sunday: nobody works (except a few tourist jobs); pub, church, beer garden, cricket; schools and builders off.
9. [x] Proper cricket pitch and a Sunday match everyone can watch; team shirts.
10. [x] Clothes for occasions: wedding dress, cricket shirt, Sunday best.
11. [x] Pub sells lots of different drinks; sims and Sam get drunk (erratic walking, slurred speech, fights).
12. [x] Buy things from shopkeepers.
13. [x] Long-term unemployed become homeless, sleep rough, cause trouble; a lock-up (like the one on the Everton badge).
14. [x] Deaths, funerals at the church, a procession to the Lift (usually Sunday); a births and deaths register at the church.
15. [x] Rare serious crime.
16. [x] Robberies should drop once there are police (and more police = fewer).
17. [x] Events shown where they happen (marker on the map for fights, robberies, filming...); more variety; full-scale brawls.
18. [x] Escaped pig: very rare, and not always right next to Sam.
19. [x] Special guests (film stars, sports stars): photos and autographs; some secretly help Sam realise it's a show.
20. [x] Billboards with adverts; some sims break into impromptu adverts (product placement).
21. [x] Pause: everyone freezes like they've been told to; a few touch their earpiece; some glance at Sam; children spy from windows and duck.
22. [x] Sims use the upper floors of buildings.
