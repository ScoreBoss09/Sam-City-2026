HOW TO PLAY

Planning view (now)

This is the map. Pick a tool on the left, then click the ground to order things: paths, a Stockyard, a hut. Nothing gets built until you order it here.

Being Sam (TAB / OPTIONS)

Walk to the glowing Tool Rack, pick up tools, chop trees, carry timber to the sites and hold E (✕) to build. Follow the yellow star on your minimap.

Stay alive

Sam gets hungry and tired. Eat with Q (△) when carrying berries or near the Stockyard, and sleep in your hut at night.

The objective box on the right always tells you the next step.
